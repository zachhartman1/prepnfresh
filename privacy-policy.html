Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This explains what personal data Prep 'N' Fresh collects, why, and what choices you have.

Who we are

Prep 'N' Fresh, 9 Palmeira Parade, Western Esplanade, Westcliff-on-Sea, Essex, SS0 7RR. You can reach us at prepnfreshuk@gmail.com or on WhatsApp.

What we collect, and why

We only collect what's needed to take and fulfil your order. When you use Build a Box and send your order to us, this includes:

  • Your name — so we know who the order is for.
  • Your postcode — to confirm whether local delivery is available and work out the collection or delivery details.
  • Your meal and package choices — so we know what to prepare.
  • Your WhatsApp number — because that's how the order reaches us, and how we confirm it with you.

We don't ask for or store any payment card details ourselves. Card payments, if and when you make one, are handled entirely by our payment provider — we never see or keep your full card number.

We don't use this site to collect health data, and we don't need to know anything about you beyond what's listed above to prepare and deliver your food.

How your order actually reaches us

Build a Box runs entirely in your own browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere until you press "Send order on WhatsApp" — at that point, your browser opens WhatsApp with your order details pre-filled as a message, which you then choose to send. WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and that message becomes part of a normal WhatsApp conversation between you and our business number, governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Local storage on your device

While you're building a box, we save your in-progress selections (package, meals, name, postcode) in your browser's local storage, purely so you don't lose your progress if you accidentally close the tab or refresh the page. This information stays on your device — it is never transmitted to us or anyone else unless you press "Send order on WhatsApp" or complete a payment. It's cleared automatically once an order is completed, and you can clear it yourself at any time using the "Reset & start again" button.

We don't use any analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this site. The only browser storage in use is the one described above, which is strictly necessary for the ordering tool to work and doesn't require separate consent under UK data protection rules — but we're telling you about it here regardless, since transparency matters more to us than the letter of what's required.

Payment processing

Where card payment is offered on this site, it is handled by a third-party payment provider who processes your payment details directly and securely. We do not store your card number, expiry date, or CVV on our own systems at any point, and our staff never see them either.

Who we share your data with

  • WhatsApp / Meta — to receive and respond to your order, if you choose to order this way.
  • Our payment provider — only if you pay by card, and only to process that specific payment.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else for marketing or any other purpose.

How long we keep it

We keep order conversations for as long as reasonably needed to fulfil your order, handle any follow-up questions, and meet our own basic accounting/record-keeping obligations. If you'd like us to delete your information sooner, contact us and we'll action it unless we're required to keep something for a legal or accounting reason.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's inaccurate
  • Delete your personal data, where we're not required to keep it
  • Provide a copy of the data you've given us

To do any of this, email prepnfreshuk@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), if you think we've handled your data incorrectly.

Changes to this policy

If how we handle your data changes — for example, once card payments go live — we'll update this page and change the date at the top.
