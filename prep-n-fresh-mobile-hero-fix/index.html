Why Prep 'N' Fresh

Meal prep that doesn't taste like meal prep

Most macro-friendly food is dry chicken and sad rice. Ours is cooked with restaurant technique — then weighed to the gram so your tracker stays honest.

Restaurant technique, everyday food

Proper stocks, brines, slow braises and sauces built from scratch. The things that make food taste like a restaurant, applied to Tuesday lunch.

Macros you can actually trust

Every component is weighed and calculated from the recipe spec. Calories, protein, carbs and fat are printed on the pot — no guessing.

Allergens made obvious

Full ingredients on every label with the 14 regulated allergens emphasised. Tell us your allergy and we'll talk it through before you order.

Restaurant technique. Built for every day.

Trained where the details matter

Our kitchen is run by a chef who spent years in fine-dining kitchens, where a sauce is tasted twenty times and a portion is weighed before it leaves the pass.

That's the same discipline behind every tray we pack. The difference is what it's aimed at: food you can eat five days a week that still hits your numbers — not a tasting menu.
