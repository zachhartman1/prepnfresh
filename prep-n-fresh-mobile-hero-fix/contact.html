FAQs

Common questions

How do I place an order?

Build your box online at any time, or message us on WhatsApp with your goal or dish picks and we'll confirm your box, total and collection or delivery slot.

Do I need a subscription?

No. There's no subscription or lock-in — order the Taster Box, Weekly Plan or Monthly Plan as and when you need it.

What if I have an allergy?

Message us before you order and we'll talk through the recipe with you. All 14 regulated allergens are declared on every dish, but the kitchen handles all of them, so we can't guarantee any dish is completely free from traces.

How long do meals keep?

Meals are best eaten within 4 days of collection, chilled. Use-by dates are printed on every label, and meals can be frozen on the day of collection for up to a month.

Can I mix and match dishes?

Yes — Build Your Box lets you choose any combination of the week's dishes, including swapping to the vegetarian option.

Do you deliver outside Westcliff?

We deliver locally across Leigh-on-Sea, Chalkwell, Westcliff and Southend from £3.50. Message us your postcode and we'll confirm.

Still have a question?

Message us on WhatsApp — it's the fastest way to reach the kitchen.
